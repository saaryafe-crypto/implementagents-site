Missed Call Text Back for Plumbers: Texts, Triage and Costs

Missed call text back sends an automatic text the moment your shop misses a call, so a homeowner with water on the floor hears from you before they dial the next plumber. This guide covers copy-paste texts for common plumbing calls, a triage table for which replies to call back first, software with prices, and the TCPA rules to follow.

What one missed plumbing call actually costs

SkipCalls, a call tracking company, reports that home service businesses miss about 45% of inbound calls and that 85% of callers who do not reach a person will not call back. The same source says 78% of customers hire the first business that responds.

Plumbing tickets range widely. Plumbing Snapshot for GHL cites a basic service call around $150 and a water heater replacement around $1,340 (sourcing Angi). A whole-home repipe can run $6,000 to $15,000 (sourcing HomeGuide). Even at the low end, a few saved calls per month add up fast.

Plumbing texts that get a reply

Keep each text about the call they just made. No coupons, no offers, no review requests. Ask one question so the reply gives you enough to call back ready. Plumbing Snapshot for GHL notes that texts have a 98% open rate (industry benchmark) and that contacting a lead within 5 minutes makes you roughly 100 times more likely to connect than waiting 30 minutes (citing MIT and InsideSales research).

ScenarioText to copy
Business hoursHi, this is [Shop Name] Plumbing. Sorry we missed your call. What do you need help with: leak, drain, water heater, or something else? Reply with your address and we will call you right back. Reply STOP to opt out.
After hours (active leak)Hi, this is [Shop Name] Plumbing. If water is running, shut off the main valve (usually near the meter or where the line enters your house). Reply with your address and what is leaking, and our on call plumber will call you back. Reply STOP to opt out.
Water heaterHi, this is [Shop Name] Plumbing. Sorry we missed you. Is this about a water heater (no hot water, leaking, or noise)? Reply with your address and what is happening and we will get back to you. Reply STOP to opt out.
Freeze seasonHi, this is [Shop Name] Plumbing. If you have a frozen or burst pipe, turn off the main water valve right away. Text us your address and which pipe is affected. We will call you back. Reply STOP to opt out.

Only promise a callback time you can keep. If your after hours plan is next morning, say that plainly.

Which replies to call back first

Not every plumbing text is equally urgent. Water damage gets worse by the minute, while a dripping faucet can wait until morning. Give whoever handles replies this table so they know what to prioritize.

Caller saysCall back withinWhy
Water running, flooding, burst pipe, sewage backup5 minutesActive water causes damage to drywall, flooring and belongings every minute it runs
No hot water, water heater leaking15 minutesUrgent but not flooding. A leaking tank can escalate, so do not wait hours
Slow drain, clogged toilet (not overflowing)1 hourAnnoying but stable. The caller is still shopping, so speed wins the job
Dripping faucet, running toilet, minor fixtureSame day or next morningLow urgency. A fast reply still beats competitors who never respond

TCPA rules for plumbing text backs

ConnectFirst, a telecom provider, states that texting someone who called your business first is not "unsolicited" under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act. Plura.ai adds that a missed inbound call establishes implied consent for a single transactional reply, citing 47 CFR 64.1200(a)(2). But that consent covers only a reply about the call itself.

  • Keep it about the call. No offers, no upsells, no seasonal promos in the auto text.
  • Include opt out language. Every automated text should end with "Reply STOP to opt out." Plura.ai notes the FCC requires opt out requests to be honored within 10 business days, but best practice is to process them immediately.
  • No drip campaigns without written consent. Do not add the caller to any marketing list or automated follow up sequence unless they agreed in writing.
  • Register your number. US carriers require A2P 10DLC registration for business texting from a 10 digit number. Start this before you turn on text back, because approval can take weeks.
  • Watch state rules. Some states have stricter hours for solicitation texts. Florida and Oklahoma cut off at 8 PM local time. Have a lawyer review your texts before launch.

Quick answers

How do I calculate missed call text back ROI?

Multiply your missed calls per month by your reply rate, then by your booking rate, then by your average ticket. Start with estimates and swap in real numbers from your text log after 30 days. Most plumbing shops cover the software cost by recovering one or two jobs per month.

Where is missed call text back in GoHighLevel?

In your sub-account, go to Settings, then Phone System, then Voice, then Voicemail and Missed Call Text Back. Toggle it on, write your message, and save. The toggle is at the sub-account level, not the agency level.

Does missed call text back replace answering the phone?

No. It catches callers who would have hung up and called someone else, but a person still has to read the reply and call back fast. For active leaks and burst pipes, a live answer or an on call plumber is still better than any text.

Is missed call text back legal?

A single text replying to a call the customer just made is generally treated as a transactional response, not unsolicited marketing. Adding offers or enrolling the caller in a drip sequence without written consent can violate the TCPA. Have a lawyer review your setup, since state rules vary.
