Housecall Pro AI Answering Service Cost: CSR AI Review (2026)

Last checked Nov 22, 2026. Prices and features change; check each provider.

Housecall Pro does not publish a price for CSR AI, its AI answering service. Its own pages call 24/7 call answering a paid add-on that is sold separately, so you get the number from sales or inside the app. You also need a Housecall Pro plan first, which runs $59 to $329 a month at list price as of November 2026.

What CSR AI costs, and what you pay before it

CSR AI is one of the AI Team helpers inside Housecall Pro. Most of the AI Team is free with every plan. The help center says all AI Team members are included for free except CSR AI, "which is an optional add-on that provides a 24/7 call answering service." The features page says CSR AI chat on your website is included on all plans, but 24/7 call answering "is a paid feature." None of the Housecall Pro pages we checked list a dollar amount, minutes included, or overage rates. The CSR AI page only says it costs "a fraction of the cost of an office admin."

So the true monthly cost is your Housecall Pro plan plus the CSR AI price you are quoted. Here are the plan prices Housecall Pro publishes.

What CSR AI does on a call

Based on Housecall Pro's feature page and help articles, here is what the answering service does today:

  • Answers calls 24/7, including nights, weekends, and holidays. You forward your business line to a number Housecall Pro gives you.
  • Books work. It can book jobs and estimates, reschedule, and cancel, using your bookable Price Book items, service area, and technician availability. You can choose to create unscheduled jobs or estimates instead of real bookings.
  • Sends you a summary after each call, with customer info and the urgency of the call, and logs it.
  • Handles emergencies. The help center says emergency handling is always on. You choose whether an emergency call is forwarded to you or triggers a text alert.
  • Hands off to a person. If a caller asks for a human, it can forward to a number you pick, pass the call to a Housecall Pro Assist live agent, or end politely and send you notes.
  • Can be tuned. You can set scripts, voice, tone, how it answers pricing questions, and how it says your business name.

Setup is mostly call forwarding plus booking settings. The help center says you get a text about an hour after you add CSR AI that walks you through forwarding.

Current limits to know before you buy

  • No published price. You cannot compare it to other answering services without a sales quote.
  • Spanish is not live yet. The CSR AI overview (July 2026) lists Spanish conversation as a coming feature.
  • Text answering is not fully live. Housecall Pro describes texts as coming soon or in Alpha.
  • It needs Housecall Pro. It books into the Housecall Pro calendar, so it only makes sense if you run your business on that software.
  • Your Price Book has to be set up. To book real jobs, line items need a duration, assignee, and price. Messy settings mean messy bookings.
  • Mixed wording on AI disclosure. The help center says it introduces itself as AI. The feature page says you can choose whether to disclose it. Ask which applies to your account.

One more point. CSR AI answers calls that customers make to you. If you ever use any tool to send automated texts or AI calls out to customers, US law (the TCPA) requires their prior written consent first.

Who it fits

CSR AI fits a shop that already runs on Housecall Pro, misses calls while on jobs or after hours, and has a clean Price Book and service area. If your main pain is missed calls at night and on weekends, and you want those calls to land straight on your calendar, this is the most direct option inside Housecall Pro.

It fits less well if you need Spanish today, if you are not on Housecall Pro, or if you want a fixed price before talking to sales.

Example

A two truck plumbing shop on the Essentials plan billed annually pays $149 a month for software. Before adding CSR AI, the owner should get the add-on price in writing, ask how many calls or minutes it covers, and ask what happens past that. Total monthly cost is $149 plus that quote. Then compare it to the cost of the jobs lost to missed calls in a normal month.

Alternatives and how prices compare

  • Housecall Pro Assist (live people). Housecall Pro also sells a human answering service add-on. It says pricing varies by coverage, such as weekday, weekend, or 24/7. No price is published.
  • Jobber AI Receptionist. If you are open to other software, Jobber lists its AI Receptionist as a $99/mo add-on on its pricing page (checked November 2026), and its Plus plan lists the receptionist among its features. No minutes or overage terms are published.
  • A traditional answering service. Housecall Pro's own guide cites $135 to $400 per month for live answering services and $0 to $200 per month for AI answering, depending on usage and features.
  • Hiring in house. The same guide cites Bureau of Labor Statistics receptionist pay of $17.90 per hour, or $37,230 per year, in 2024.

The fair test is simple. Ask each option the same questions: monthly price, calls or minutes included, overage cost, Spanish support, emergency handling, and whether it books into your calendar.

Quick answers

How much does Housecall Pro CSR AI cost per month?

Housecall Pro does not publish the price. Its pages call 24/7 call answering a paid, separately sold add-on, so you need a quote from sales or the app, on top of a plan that starts at $59 a month billed annually.

Is CSR AI included in any Housecall Pro plan?

CSR AI chat on your website is included on all plans. The 24/7 phone answering part is a paid add-on on every plan, according to Housecall Pro's AI Team page and help center.

Can CSR AI book jobs on my calendar?

Yes. The help center says it checks technician availability and can book jobs and estimates, reschedule, and cancel, based on your booking settings and Price Book.

Does CSR AI speak Spanish?

Not yet. Housecall Pro's CSR AI overview from July 2026 lists Spanish conversation as a coming feature.
