How to get past customers to book again

A customer who already paid you once is the easiest job to win again. Most shops never ask. A simple seasonal reminder fills slow weeks.

When each trade should reach out

TradeGood moment to remind
Window cleaningEarly spring and before the holidays
Carpet cleaningSpring cleaning and before Thanksgiving
HVACBefore summer for AC, before winter for heating
Garage doorsA yearly tune up, before winter
PlumbingBefore winter for frozen pipe checks, water heater flush once a year

Do it the right way

  • Email first. Legal with a clear opt out and your business address.
  • Text only with consent. Automated marketing texts and calls generally need prior written consent under the TCPA. Ask for it on your next invoice or booking.
  • Calls from your own staff. A list of who is due, called by a person, is the simplest safe option.
  • Never use AI voice calls for marketing without written consent. The FCC treats AI voices as artificial under the TCPA.

What to say

Example email

Hi Sarah, it has been a year since we cleaned your windows on Oak Street. Spring slots are opening up. Reply to this email and we will save you a time.
