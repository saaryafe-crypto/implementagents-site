HVAC Missed Call Text Back: Setup, Scripts, Rules and ROI

Missed call text back sends an automatic text to anyone whose call you did not pick up, so a no-heat caller hears from you in seconds instead of calling the next company on Google. Turn it on in your phone system or CRM, write a short text that asks what is wrong and where, and register your number so carriers deliver it. Below are ready texts, the rules, and a way to check if it pays.

Why HVAC shops miss the calls that matter most

Invoca reports that 27% of calls to home services businesses are not answered, and that less than 3% of callers who reach voicemail leave a message. For an HVAC shop the worst of those misses land at the worst times: the first cold snap, the first heat wave, Saturday night when a tech is on a roof and the office is closed.

A caller with no heat does not wait. If nobody answers and nothing comes back, they call the next number. A text that arrives while they are still looking keeps the job in play. Invoca uses a $4,500 furnace replacement as its example of what one lost call can be worth. One saved replacement a season can pay for a texting tool for a long time.

Set it up in one afternoon

  1. Find the setting. Most phone systems, call tracking tools and field service CRMs have it. In GoHighLevel (GHL) it is under Settings, Phone System, Voice, then Voicemail and Missed Call Text Back.
  2. Set the ring time. GHL only counts a call as missed after it rings past your timeout, and its help page says 10 to 20 seconds is typical. Too short and you text people your CSR was about to answer.
  3. Register your number. US carriers require businesses texting from a regular 10 digit number to register it (called A2P 10DLC). Twilio says it blocks unregistered texts, and in 2023 said approvals could take 2 to 3 weeks. Start this first.
  4. Stop duplicate texts. GHL sends a text for every missed call, even when the same person calls three times in a row. Its help page suggests adding a wait step of about 20 minutes or tagging first and second calls in a workflow.
  5. Route replies to a person. The text only works if someone reads the answer. Send replies to the on call tech's phone after hours, not just an inbox.
  6. Test it. Call from a cell phone, let it ring out, and time how long the text takes. Do it again after hours.

Copy-paste texts for HVAC calls

Keep each text about the call they just made. No coupons, no maintenance plan pitch. That keeps it a reply, not marketing.

During business hours

Hi, this is [Company]. Sorry we missed your call, we are on another line. Reply with what is going on (no heat, no cooling, noise, leak) and your address, and we will call you right back. Reply STOP to opt out.

After hours and weekends

Hi, this is [Company]. Our office is closed but our on call tech gets these texts. If you have no heat or no cooling, reply URGENT with your address and we will call you within [X] minutes. Otherwise reply with a good time tomorrow. Reply STOP to opt out.

When a booked customer calls (if your system can tell)

Hi [Name], this is [Company]. Sorry we missed you. Is this about your appointment on [day]? Reply here and we will sort it out. Reply STOP to opt out.

Only promise a callback time you can actually hit. A text that says 15 minutes followed by silence is worse than no text.

Is missed call text back legal?

A one time text that answers a call the customer just made is common and lower risk than marketing. It is not risk free. Law firm Fisher Phillips wrote in January 2026 that texting lawsuits are on the rise, that the business carries the burden of proving consent, and that simply giving a business a phone number is not enough consent for marketing texts.

  • Reply only about the call. Do not add specials, review requests or plan offers to this text.
  • Do not add that number to a promo list or drip campaign without separate written consent.
  • Honor STOP right away. Fisher Phillips advises processing opt outs in real time.
  • Keep records of calls, texts and opt outs.
  • Register your number with your texting provider.
  • State laws can be stricter than federal law. Ask a lawyer to look at your exact texts before you go live.

These numbers are made up to show the math. Use your own. A shop misses 60 calls a month. It guesses 1 in 4 reply to the text (15 people) and it books 1 in 3 of those (5 jobs). At a $400 average ticket that is $2,000 a month saved. If the tool costs $100 a month, the first job pays for it.

Quick answers

Where is missed call text back in GHL?

In the sub-account, go to Settings, Phone System, Voice, then Voicemail and Missed Call Text Back. Enable it, write your message, send a test and save.

Is missed call text back legal?

A single text replying to a call the customer just made is common, but texting law is strict. Keep the text about the call, honor STOP, keep records, and have a lawyer review it, since state rules vary.

Does it replace answering the phone?

No. It saves some callers who would have hung up, but a person still has to call back fast. For no-heat and no-cool emergencies, a live answer or an on call tech is still best.

Does it work for plumbers and contractors?

Yes, the setup is the same. Change the text to list the problems your callers have, like leaks, water heaters or broken springs.
