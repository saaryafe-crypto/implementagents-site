How much do missed calls cost a home service business?

Every unanswered call is a customer who goes down the list on Google and calls the next company. You can work out what that costs you with four numbers you already know.

The two minute math

Take one normal month and fill in your own numbers:

  1. Calls you missed (your phone log or call tracking shows it)
  2. How many of them were new customers (a fair guess is fine)
  3. Your close rate on new callers who reach you
  4. Your average job value

Lost each month = missed new callers × close rate × average job

Example, not real data

A shop misses 30 calls in a month. 20 are new customers. It closes 40% of callers it reaches, and the average job is $350. That is 20 × 0.40 × $350 = $2,800 a month, before any repeat work from those customers.

How many calls do businesses really miss?

Published figures differ a lot, and most come from phone and software vendors. Call tracking company CallRail has reported that home service businesses miss about 14% of calls, and about 28% across all the businesses on its platform. Older research by MightyCall found that local home service companies believed they answered almost every call but actually answered about two thirds. A fair working range is one in seven to one in four calls. Your own phone log is the only number that really counts.

When calls get missed

Mostly when you are busy doing the work: on a roof, under a sink, driving between jobs, after 5 pm and on weekends. That is also when emergency jobs come in, and emergency callers rarely leave a voicemail. They call the next number.

Three ways to stop the leak

  • Text back every missed call within a minute. Many callers answer a text even when they would not leave a voicemail.
  • Have every call answered. A person, an answering service, or an AI receptionist that can book the job.
  • Track it. Count missed calls every week. What gets counted gets fixed.
