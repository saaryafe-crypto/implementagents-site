How to follow up on estimates that did not close

Many customers who get a price do not say no. They get busy, compare, or forget. A short, polite follow up wins back a share of them, and most shops never send one.

A follow up plan you can copy

WhenWhat
Same daySend the quote in writing with one clear next step: reply yes to book.
Day 2 or 3Check in: any questions about the quote?
Day 7Offer the next open slots on your schedule.
Day 14Last note: the quote stays open until a date you name.

What to say

Example message

Hi Maria, this is Dan from Summit Garage Doors. Just checking if you had any questions about the spring repair quote. We have openings Thursday and Friday morning if you want to get it done. Reply here and I will book it.

  • Use their name and the actual job.
  • Offer real time slots, not a vague call me.
  • Keep it short. One question, one next step.
  • Stop after the last note. No pressure messages.

Stay within the rules

A follow up on a quote the customer asked for is part of the job they requested. Marketing messages are different: in the US, automated marketing texts and calls generally need the customer's prior written consent under the TCPA. Email follow ups need an easy way to opt out under CAN SPAM. When in doubt, email, or have your own staff call.
