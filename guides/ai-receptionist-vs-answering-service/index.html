AI receptionist or answering service: which is right for a small contractor?

Three kinds of help can answer your phone. They differ in price, in what they can do with the call, and in how much setup they leave to you.

What a live service does well

A real person on every call, good for upset or complex callers. The catch is cost per call and that most services take a message instead of booking, so you still have to call back.

What an AI receptionist does well

Answers every call at once, day and night, for a small part of the cost, and can book on your calendar. The catch is setup: scripts, prices, service area and calendar rules are yours to configure and keep up to date.

Questions to ask any provider

  • Does it book the job, or only take a message?
  • Does the job reach my technicians automatically?
  • What happens after the call: reminders, follow ups, closing the job?
  • Who sets it up, and who fixes it when something changes?
  • Can I leave easily, and do I keep my customer list?

Quick answers

How much does an answering service cost for a contractor?

Live answering services often cost about $10 to $12 per call, with plans from about $250 a month. AI receptionists often cost about $1 to $2 per call. Prices change, so check each provider.

Can an AI receptionist book jobs?

Many can book on a connected calendar. Ask whether the job also reaches your technicians and is followed up after the call.
