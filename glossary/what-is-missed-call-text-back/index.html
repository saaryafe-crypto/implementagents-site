What Is Missed Call Text Back? A Plain Guide for Service Owners

Missed call text back is an automation that watches your business line for unanswered, declined, or voicemail calls and sends the caller a text from your business number within seconds. The caller can reply, and the conversation continues by text.

Why it matters to a home service owner

When a homeowner has a leaking water heater, they rarely wait for a callback. They dial the next number in the search results, often without leaving a voicemail. Vendasta cites a CallRail survey in which 82% of people said they would call a competitor if a business did not answer. Treat that as a survey claim.

A text back does not answer the call. ZyraTalk, a vendor in this space, calls it a recovery tool, not a coverage tool. It gives the customer a reason to wait for you.

How it works

  1. A call to your business number goes unanswered or to voicemail.
  2. Your phone system sends a short text from your business number within seconds.
  3. The customer's reply lands in your team inbox, and you text or call back.

Business phone services and field service software offer this as a setting. A plain iPhone does not. Apple's guide says you can decline a call and pick a text reply, but only by hand each time.

Example

Example

A plumber under a sink misses a call. Seconds later the caller gets: "Hi, this is Smith Plumbing. Sorry we missed your call. We are on a job. Reply with your address and the problem and we will get back to you shortly. Reply STOP to opt out." The caller replies "water heater leaking in garage." The office calls back and books the job.

Limits and rules to know

  • Carrier registration. Twilio says business texts sent from an app over a local number to US phones need A2P 10DLC registration. ZyraTalk warns unregistered texts can be filtered while your dashboard still shows them as sent.
  • Keep it a reply, not an ad. Vendasta notes no FCC order says a call is, by itself, consent to an automated text. Get prior written consent under the TCPA before any marketing texts.
  • Honor STOP. Stop texting anyone who asks.
  • Emergencies and non texters. Some callers want a human now, and some ignore texts from unknown numbers.

General information, not legal advice. Texting rules changed in 2026.

Related terms

  • Answering service: live people who pick up when you cannot.
  • A2P 10DLC: US carrier registration for business texting from a local number.
  • TCPA: the federal law on consent for automated calls and texts.
  • AI receptionist: software that answers the call itself.

Quick answers

Is it legal to text someone back after they call my business?

A single text that replies to their call and names your business is generally treated as a defensible use, but no FCC order says the call alone is consent. Keep it about their call, include STOP wording, and get prior written consent before sending any marketing.

Can I set this up on my regular cell phone?

Not fully on an iPhone. Apple lets you decline a call and choose a text reply by hand, but automatic texting after every missed call needs a business phone service or service software.

Why are customers not replying to my text back?

Check that your number is registered for business texting (A2P 10DLC). Unregistered texts can be filtered by carriers without any error showing on your side.
