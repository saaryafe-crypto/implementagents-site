Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Implement Agents ("we", "us") builds AI assistants that run office work for home service businesses. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it and the choices you have. It covers our website, implementagents.com, and our WhatsApp office assistant.

Who this applies to

  • Our clients: business owners and their staff who use our assistant, including on WhatsApp.
  • Website visitors: people who visit implementagents.com or fill in our contact form.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, company name and email when you give them to us.
  • WhatsApp messages: when you message our WhatsApp number, we receive your phone number or WhatsApp user ID, your profile name, and the messages and files you send and receive.
  • Business data you connect: if your business connects tools such as a calendar, email or job management software, we access the data needed to do the tasks you ask for, such as jobs, appointments and customer contact details.
  • Technical data: basic logs such as message times and delivery status, used to keep the service running.

How we use it

  • To answer your messages and do the office tasks you ask for.
  • To keep track of your conversation so the assistant has context.
  • To fix problems, prevent abuse and improve the service.
  • To contact you about your account or a request you made.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your messages for advertising.

Each business is kept separate

Every client business has its own data, history and connected tools. Our assistant only ever works with the data of the business you belong to, and one business can never see another business's data.

Who we share it with

We share data only with providers that help us run the service, and only as needed:

  • Meta (WhatsApp): delivers messages between you and our assistant, under WhatsApp's business terms.
  • AI model providers: your messages and the related business data are processed by AI models to write replies. These providers process the data on our behalf.
  • Hosting and communications providers: to store data and run our phone and messaging services.

We may also share data if the law requires it or to protect the safety of people or our service.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages and data while your business uses our service. When a business stops using the service, we delete its data within 90 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Security

We protect data with access controls and encryption in transit, and we limit who on our team can access it. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your data at any time. Email saar@implementagents.com with your name and the phone number you use on WhatsApp. We will confirm and delete your data within 30 days. You can also stop receiving messages from us at any time by replying "stop".

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, and to object to how we use it. Email us to use any of these rights.

Children

Our service is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we change the date at the top of this page.
