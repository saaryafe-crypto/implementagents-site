Home services

Bringing back every customer when they are due

Carpets need cleaning every year. Windows every season. Garage doors and AC units need a tune up. Your past customers will buy again, if someone reminds them.

The problem

Most home service businesses have hundreds or thousands of past customers in their software. Nobody has time to call them when they are due, so they book whoever reaches them first.

What it does

  • Knows when each customer is due again, based on their last job.
  • Reaches them by text, email or call with a friendly reminder.
  • Books the job straight into your schedule.
  • Renews maintenance plans and wins back customers who lapsed.

What it looks like

Example text thread

AI: Hi Linda, it has been a year since we cleaned your carpets. Want me to book this year's cleaning?

Customer: Yes, maybe next week?

AI: I have Tuesday at 10 or Thursday at 2. Which works?

Your first 30 days
