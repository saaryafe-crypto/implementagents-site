Reaching every client before renewal

Clients leave when nobody talks to them until the renewal bill shows up.

The problem

Producers focus on new business, so renewals often go out with no conversation. Rate increases surprise clients and retention slips.

What the agent does

  • Contacts clients 60 to 90 days before renewal.
  • Asks about changes: new car, new driver, home updates, a new business.
  • Flags clients at risk of leaving so a producer can step in.
  • Books review calls automatically.

What it looks like

Example text thread

Agent: Hi James, your home policy renews on March 3. Anything changed this year, like a new roof or a pool?

Client: We added a pool actually.

Agent: Good to know, that can affect your coverage. Want me to book 15 minutes with Lisa this week?
